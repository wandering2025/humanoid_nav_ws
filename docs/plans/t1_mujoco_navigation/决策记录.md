## ADR-026 W16 rehearsal: retain scans until exact evaluation TF arrives (2026-10-20)

The first capture/encode-load rehearsal reached B and waited 17.92s with 34 obstacle-marked local costmap frames, but its evaluator retained zero scan records during the entire active obstacle interval and returned FAILED. The independent mapping log contains 224 frames / 9352 original ray endpoints inside the observed physical obstacle during that interval. This corroborates sensing; the original FAILED result is retained.

The old evaluator attempted exact-time TF once in the scan callback and silently discarded the sample if TF or obstacle callbacks had not arrived. W16 now opts into an evaluator-only queue: at most 25 original scans with their original Collision Monitor context, at most 0.5 wall seconds waiting, polled every 0.05s. Matching still requires the scan's original timestamp, the unchanged obstacle timestamp tolerance and the same geometric hit predicate. Missing evidence expires and is counted, never replaced with latest TF or synthesized hits. Counters record received, sampled, expired, overflow and missing-TF/obstacle attempts. The default legacy Probe path remains immediate.

This changes observation/evaluation delivery only. SLAM, raw LiDAR, explorer, Nav2, safety, event timing and physical walking are unchanged. No evaluator information flows back to the robot. Validation covers delayed original-stamp TF, original raw endpoints/CM context, exactly-once sampling, expiry and bounded overflow, existing CPU suite, and the full final rehearsal matrix restarted with this evaluator. Prior five successful approach-version baselines and the failed load run remain historical evidence, not relabeled final-version successes.

Follow-up diagnosis: the 0.5s queue alone failed again (deferred-share-01). Bounded exact TF exception logging in tf-diagnostic-01 measured a request at 31.8s while the evaluator TF buffer had only reached 29.2s. This is a stale transport backlog, not permission to use a newer transform. W16 now uses a depth-20 /tf subscription instead of the legacy depth-1000 queue; tf2 retains its timestamped transform history and every ray is still matched at its original scan stamp. Legacy callers retain depth 1000. Bounded error samples and queue depth are saved with results. The diagnostic also hit a real physical scan gap of 0.307602s at sim 28.1s and latched the unchanged 0.3s safety timeout; cause of that scheduling delay is not yet established. It stopped safely and remains FAILED, not a successful cancellation. Capture then exited when windows closed. No safety threshold or capture workload is reduced. Final matrix remains pending until this version passes under the same load.

## ADR-025 W16 live rehearsal: physical approach-triggered EXP01 (2026-10-20)

Repeated GUI rehearsal found an encounter-timing defect: original-base-02 reached B safely but EXP01 had already withdrawn before the explorer selected its B route. The original x>=10 trigger fired while travelling through an exploratory target at y≈7.66, heading≈−0.44 rad. Waiting was 0s, so the complete dynamic-demo result remains FAILED.

EXP01 requires y∈[5.6,7.4]m and heading within 0.65rad of south, together with the existing x>=10, READY, enabled and forward-command conditions. Robot and event physical stepping, obstacle geometry/speed/12s hold, safety chain and success criteria remain unchanged. Other scenarios without the optional condition preserve their original trigger.

This is a controlled encounter scenario, not an adversarial obstacle or a claim of universal dynamic robustness. It avoids changing robot exploration to make the scene look repeatable. Validation: reject malformed trigger conditions; replay both original measured trajectories against the new trigger to check timing; event owner/reset/interpolation/legacy tests; four-package build; restart the preregistered 5 default + 5 capture/encode proxy GUI acceptance matrix on the new version, plus cancellation/restart checks.
