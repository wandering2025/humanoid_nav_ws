Boundary inner faces: x=[-2,8], y=[-5,5], walls 0.20 m thick and 1.50 m tall. Boxes are 1.50 m high and rest at z=0. Ground z=0, contact/friction unchanged from W05.

Fresh FK data is computed only on a separate MjData copy; active physics has one owner. All body origins/rotations, including head, two hands and two feet, agree: maximum position error 1.18e-11 m; maximum rotation-matrix element error 9.95e-09. Every replay matches preserved W05 x/y/z/yaw/roll/pitch/velocities exactly (maximum error 0). Original raw trace hash and source manifest are in description-envelope.json.
